# The Upside Down

A quiet night street that turns over into its dark mirror world: the sky goes red, spores
drift through the air, vines creep out of the ground, and red lightning cracks overhead.

- The area starts in the Upside Down, with the street hanging upside down from the top of
  the window.
- **Space** flips between the normal world and the Upside Down. The street turns over
  through its middle over about a second.
- In the normal world: stars, a moon, lit windows, and a steady string of coloured lights.
- In the Upside Down: the stars and moon are gone, red storm clouds glow, windows go dark,
  the lights flicker and fail, vines grow up from the ground, pale spores float upward, and
  lightning strikes on its own every 2 to 6 seconds.
- A **click** strikes lightning down to the pointer, in either world. The lights flare with
  each strike.
- Typing a **letter key** (A to Z) makes the bulb for that letter, and its neighbours, blaze
  for a moment.
- The scene fills the whole window and is rebuilt when the window changes size.
