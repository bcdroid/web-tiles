# Touchdown Spike

A football player runs into the end zone, raises the ball, and spikes it. The ball bounces
off the turf in an unpredictable direction, confetti falls, and the player jumps to
celebrate. The scene then starts over by itself, about every 7 seconds.

- **Space** or a **click** starts the run over right away.
- Every bounce of the ball is a little different, because a football's shape makes it
  bounce at random angles.
- The scene is drawn for a 1280 by 800 window and scaled to fit any window size, so it looks
  the same full screen and in a gallery tile.
