# Tic Tac Toe

Two players take turns on a 3 by 3 board. Every mark is animated as it is drawn.

- The grid draws itself line by line when a game starts.
- Player 1 is X and goes first; Player 2 is O. Click an empty square to place your mark.
  Clicks are ignored while a mark is still being drawn.
- The text above the board says whose turn it is.
- Three in a row (across, down, or diagonal) wins: a yellow line is drawn through the row and
  the message says which player won.
- If all nine squares fill with no winner, the board shakes, the marks fade, and the message
  says it is a tie.
- **Play Again** clears the board for a new two-player game.
- **Watch Auto Play** makes the computer play both sides, one move a second, until someone
  wins or it is a tie. It usually takes a winning move or blocks one, but not always.
- There are no keyboard controls.
